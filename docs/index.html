The boring, important bit

Your phone, your data

Nothing leaves your phone

The app has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. No account, no ads, no analytics.

Doesn't break your bank apps

UPI and bank apps refuse to run while accessibility apps are on. Thumb Tally switches itself off when one opens and reminds you to turn it back on.

Only looks at three apps

It reads the screen only while YouTube, Instagram or Snapchat is open, to tell which video is playing. Never your messages or what you type.

Light on battery

Asleep everywhere else. Inside those apps it checks about once a second.

Questions

Fair questions

Why does it need accessibility access?

It's the only way Android lets an app see that you swiped to a new video in another app. Thumb Tally uses it to read the creator and caption of the video on screen, only inside YouTube, Instagram and Snapchat.

Is it safe to install from outside the Play Store?

The code is open source, so anyone can check what it does. It has no internet permission. Android shows the same warnings for every app that isn't from the Play Store.

Does it count regular YouTube videos?

It tracks them separately, as time, and they never count towards your daily limit. The counter is for Shorts, Reels and Spotlight.

What about iPhone?

Apple doesn't let apps see inside other apps, so counting videos isn't possible there. Android only for now.

How do I update or uninstall?

Download the new APK from this page and install it over the old one; your history stays. On some phones (OnePlus especially) switch it off and on in Accessibility after an update. Uninstalling deletes everything.
